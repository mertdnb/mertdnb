About

I’m Mert, Founder & Lead Developer at Vesors, based in Türkiye. I build mobile applications, modern websites and AI-powered products, taking projects from the first idea through development and launch.

My work brings together product development and digital solutions, with Vesors and Vesors Digital.

• Mobile development — Flutter applications backed by Firebase.
• Web development — responsive interfaces and business applications.
• AI integration — image analysis, analytics and automation.
• Cloud architecture — APIs and backend services with Cloudflare Workers.
